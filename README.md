## How I work

- **Listen first.** The best solution starts with understanding people and context.
- **Reduce complexity.** I break large problems into smaller, practical steps.
- **Ship and learn.** Small releases and real feedback lead to better systems.
- **Be transparent.** I communicate constraints, decisions and timing clearly.
- **Keep it simple.** A well-designed system should feel easy, even when it is complex underneath.
